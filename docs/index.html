Built like it means it

Every bypass you can think of was designed against.

🚫No snooze, ever

The ringing screen renders zero buttons. There is nothing to tap, swipe, or negotiate with.

📷Camera-verified reps

On-device pose detection tracks your joints. Half-reps and fast flailing don't count.

🔒Lockdown mode

Lock-task pins the ringing screen. The phone is a single-purpose rep-counting machine until you're done.

🔁Survives reboots

Exact alarms, re-armed after restarts, dead batteries, and clock changes. The debt never expires.

🎯Four workouts

Squats, push-ups, jumping jacks, sit-ups — each with its own joint-angle model and tempo window.

🧪Practice mode

Test your camera framing and the counter with zero stakes. No alarm, no lockdown.

Your mornings are yours

Strict doesn't mean surveillance.

  • ✕No video ever leaves your phone. Frames are analyzed on-device; only rep counts cross into the app.
  • ✕No account. No sign-up, no login, no cloud sync.
  • ✕No tracking. No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs phoning home.
  • ✕Local storage only. Alarms in SQLite, settings in MMKV — all on your device.
